What is CPM?

CPM stands for cost per mille — Latin for "cost per thousand." It is the price an advertiser pays for every 1,000 impressions (views) of an ad. CPM is the most common pricing model for awareness and reach-focused campaigns across display, social, video, and programmatic advertising.

The formulas

CPM = (Campaign Cost ÷ Impressions) × 1,000
Campaign Cost = (CPM × Impressions) ÷ 1,000
Impressions = (Campaign Cost ÷ CPM) × 1,000

Example

If you spend $2,000 on a campaign that delivers 500,000 impressions, your CPM is (2,000 ÷ 500,000) × 1,000 = $4.00. In other words, you pay $4 for every thousand times your ad is shown.
